# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Be constructive about code and ideas, accept corrections, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks or publishing another person's private information.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, limit participation or ban repeat offenders. Apply rules consistently, consider context, and explain moderation decisions where privacy permits. Do not retaliate against good-faith reports.

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through an available private contact channel. No personal contact address has been configured for this new local repository; maintainers must establish one before opening a public community. Do not put sensitive reports in public issues.
